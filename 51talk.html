What is 51Talk?

Asia's leading online English platform.

51Talk (NYSE: COE) is one of Asia's largest and most trusted online English education platforms. It connects thousands of English learners, primarily in China, with certified Filipino English teachers for one-on-one lessons.

As an official 51Talk partner, AID Virtual helps qualified Filipinos navigate the application and onboarding process, increasing your chances of acceptance and setting you up for long-term teaching success.

Our guidance is completely free. We succeed when our teachers succeed.

Am I qualified?

51Talk teacher requirements.

These requirements are preserved from the existing site and should remain unchanged unless 51Talk updates its official rules.

Basic qualifications

  • Bachelor's Degree (any field; Education is a plus but not required)
  • Strong English Communication Skills
  • Stable Internet Connection (minimum 10 Mbps wired recommended)
  • Quiet Teaching Environment
  • Laptop or Desktop Computer with HD webcam and headset
  • Guimaras-Based (currently limited to this region)

What AID Virtual helps with

  • Document preparation and application steps
  • Demo class coaching and practical feedback
  • Profile setup and onboarding guidance
  • Community support after approval

Location note: New Poblacion, Buenavista, Guimaras is called out on the current 51Talk page as a trust signal.
